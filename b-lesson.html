COMPLEX NUMBERS / FOUNDATIONS

Beyond the
real numbers.

A new number system. The same care with algebra.

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN

A clear destination.

  • Use i² = −1 to solve equations with no real roots.
  • Read, compare and calculate with Cartesian complex numbers.
  • Explain the scope of algebraic rules and justify complex solutions.

Success criterion: I can reduce powers of i and find both roots of x²+k=0.

01 / UNDERSTAND

A definition that opens the door.

The real equation \(x^2+1=0\) has no real solution because real squares are nonnegative. Define \(i\) by \(i^2=-1\). Then \(i\) and \(-i\) both solve it. We work in \(\mathbb C=\{a+ib:a,b\in\mathbb R\}\). The usual distributive and associative laws still hold; whenever \(i^2\) appears, replace it with −1.

WORKED EXAMPLE · METHOD & MEANING

For \(i^{30}\), write \(30=4(7)+2\). Since \(i^4=1\), \(i^{30}=(i^4)^7i^2=-1\). For \(x^2+32=0\), \(x=\pm4\sqrt2\,i\); square either root to check.

CORE PRACTICE

Try it. Then explain it.

Find \((3-2i)^2\) in Cartesian form. Work by hand first, then use Check expression to compare your interpretation.
